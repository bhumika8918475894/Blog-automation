### URL Anatomy (Illustrative)  

```
https://www.scaler.com/dsml-freetrial?
utm_source=bing
&utm_medium=cpc
&utm_campaign=perf_scaler-dsml_freetrial_ads_bing_pmaxbing-audience_1_key-metros_25-45
&param1=…
&param2=…
&param3=…
&msclkid=cffb63a6137b18856eb0093a5a92ee9d
```

- **utm_source=bing** – confirms the traffic originates from Bing’s search network.  
- **utm_medium=cpc** – indicates a paid click model, useful for ROI calculations.  
- **utm_campaign** – a long‑form identifier that encodes the product (DSML free trial), ad platform (Bing), bidding strategy (pmaxbing), audience (metro, 25‑45), and the internal campaign name.  
- **param1‑param3** – placeholder query strings that can carry additional segmentation data (e.g., device type, ad variant).  
- **msclkid** – Microsoft’s click‑ID used for post‑click attribution in Bing Ads.

These parameters are not visible to the user but are logged on the server side, allowing Scaler to answer questions such as:

- Which metropolitan region generated the highest registration rate?  
- How does click‑through performance differ between ad creatives (param1‑param3 variations)?  
- What is the cost per qualified lead for the 25‑45 demographic?
